About SimpleKit

Simple tools. Smarter everyday tasks.

What is SimpleKit?

SimpleKit is a collection of free online tools designed to make everyday tasks easier.

Whether you need to calculate a percentage, count words, work out a discount, convert units or calculate an average, SimpleKit brings useful tools together in one convenient place.

Our goal

Our goal is to make useful online tools accessible to everyone. We believe everyday tasks should be simple, quick and straightforward.

What we offer

  • Free percentage calculator
  • Free word counter
  • Free discount calculator
  • Free unit converter
  • Free average calculator

Easy to use

SimpleKit is designed with simplicity in mind. Our tools work directly in your web browser, so you don't need to install software or create an account.
