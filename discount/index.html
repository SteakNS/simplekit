Discount Calculator

Find out how much you save and what the final sale price will be after a discount.

How to use it

Enter the original price and discount percentage. SimpleKit calculates the amount saved and the final price.

Example calculation

Imagine an item costs 80 and has a 25% discount.

  • Original price: 80
  • Discount: 25%
  • Money saved: 20
  • Final price: 60
