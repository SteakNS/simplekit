Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SimpleKit. This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit and use this website.

2. Information you enter into our tools

SimpleKit's calculators and other tools are designed to process the information you enter directly in your browser. The tools do not intentionally send your calculator inputs to a SimpleKit server.

For your privacy, avoid entering passwords, financial account details, or other sensitive personal information into website tools.

3. Theme preference

SimpleKit may save your light or dark theme preference in your browser's local storage so the website can remember your choice on your next visit.

4. Cookies and analytics

SimpleKit does not currently intentionally use its own analytics service or advertising cookies. This may change if analytics or advertising services are added in the future. If that happens, this policy will be updated to explain relevant data collection and third-party practices.

5. Third-party websites

Some links may take you to third-party websites, such as GitHub. Those websites have their own privacy policies and practices. SimpleKit does not control how they handle information.

6. Children's privacy

SimpleKit is a general-purpose website and is not intended to collect personal information from visitors. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through external services linked from this website.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as SimpleKit changes. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can contact the project through the SimpleKit GitHub Issues page.

This policy is general information, not legal advice. Review and update it if you add advertising, analytics, forms, or other services.
