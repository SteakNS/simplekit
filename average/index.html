Average Calculator

Calculate the mean, median, sum and number of values in your list.

Separate numbers using commas, spaces or new lines.

What does it calculate?

SimpleKit gives you four useful statistical results.

📊

Mean

The sum of all numbers divided by their count.

🔢

Median

The middle value after sorting the numbers.

➕

Sum

The total when all numbers are added together.

🧮

Count

The number of values entered into the calculator.

Example

Enter these numbers:

10, 20, 30, 40, 50

  • Mean: 30
  • Median: 30
  • Sum: 150
  • Count: 5
