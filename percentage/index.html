Percentage Calculator

Calculate percentages, find percentage changes and solve everyday percentage problems.

How to use this calculator

Select the type of percentage calculation, enter your two numbers and press Calculate to see your result.

Example

To find 20% of 150, select "What is X% of Y?", enter 20 and 150, then calculate.

The answer is 30.
